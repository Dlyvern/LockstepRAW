Gunshots (player and NPC, pitched variants): gunshot.mp3 supplied by the project owner.
Impacts: Impacts/Gun/sfx (CC0) https://opengameart.org/content/impactsgunsfx
Reload, metal, hits: 100 CC0 SFX by rubberduck (CC0) https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx
Ambience: 100 CC0 SFX #2 by rubberduck (CC0) https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx-2
Footsteps: "Footsteps on different surfaces" (CC-BY 3.0) https://opengameart.org/content/footsteps-on-different-surfaces (contains sounds by freesound.org authors, see the pack licence files)
Siren, heartbeat, fluorescent buzz and crackles (ambience/fluorescent_*): synthesised.
Rain (ambience/rain_outdoor, ambience/rain_indoor) and thunder (ambience/thunder_*): synthesised (ffmpeg noise generators).
Glass breaking (impacts/glass_break_*): synthesised (noise crash and sine tinkles, Python + ffmpeg).
Flashlight click, fire mode switch (weapons/flashlight_click, weapons/firemode_switch), held breath and exhale (player/breath_hold, player/breath_release): synthesised (ffmpeg).
Reload parts (weapons/v45_mag_out, v45_mag_in, v45_charge): cut from the rubberduck CC0 pack above (metal_04, switch_02, spring_03).
Casings (casings/*), dropped magazines (magazines/*), gunshot tails (tails/*): synthesised (Content/source/scripts/make_weapon_fx_sounds.py; tails convolve the firearms library transient with generated room/echo responses).
Vault (player/vault_*): synthesised (Content/source/scripts/make_vault_sounds.py: hand slap, cloth scuff, gear rattle).
Kick (player/kick_swing_*, player/kick_hit_*): synthesised (Content/source/scripts/make_kick_sounds.py: air and cloth whoosh, boot thud).
M4 and Glock gunshots (weapons/m4_shot_*, weapons/glock_shot_*): pitch and filter variants of the V-45 gunshots above (Content/source/scripts/make_loadout_shots.py).
Startup intro (intro/*): synthesised (Content/source/scripts/make_intro_sounds.py: drone, room air, gust, pad, body-cam static and beeps).
