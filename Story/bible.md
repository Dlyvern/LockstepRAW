# Story bible

Status: draft. Everything marked **OPEN** waits for a decision from the user.

## Premise

Two broke patrol cops share a shabby flat in a rundown American district. They work the
worst-funded precinct in a fictional city. The game starts as a grounded cop story; horror
creeps in later and never announces itself early.

## The leads (Player 1 and Player 2)

Flatmates, partners, same rank (patrol officers), same pay (not enough). Lines in the game
address them together ("you two") or work for either one; neither is "the main one".

- **Player 1** - the one who keeps the flat (barely) running: pays the bills late, fixes the
  boiler with tape. Patient, dry, notices things.
  Name options (**OPEN**): Dan Harlow / Eddie Marsh / Tom Kessel
- **Player 2** - the one who's always one bad week from quitting. Quick mouth, good shot, bad
  with money. Loyal past the point of sense.
  Name options (**OPEN**): Nick Varga / Joe Lindqvist / Ray Okafor

Both are men in the current draft because the chief's lines say "gentlemen". **OPEN:** confirm,
or say if either lead should be a woman (the lines change).

## The chief

Chief of the precinct, 50s, office on the 2nd floor that's too big for the furniture in it.
Sarcastic about money because he's the one who fights for it. Cares about his people and
would rather die than say so. Placeholder name: **Chief Roy Calder** (**OPEN**).

## The station (14th Precinct)

Lived-in and worth caring about: it suffers a tragedy later, so the people here must be
specific now.

- **Desk sergeant** (lobby) - 31 years at the desk, 29 days to retirement, dreams of a lake.
- **Armorer** (basement cage) - counts every round, proud of a daughter on the honor roll
  (photo taped to the cage).
- **Range officer** (6-lane range) - 20 years on the range, half deaf, gruff.
- **Holding guard** (basement cells) - buys prisoners sandwiches with his own money.
- **Bullpen** - tired officers, frozen overtime, a broken copier, Rodriguez bringing cake Friday.
- **Prisoners** - drunks, petty thieves, one man who keeps asking the time.

**OPEN:** names for the sergeant, armorer, range officer and guard (left unnamed in dialogue
for now so they can be named once).

## Weapons

Fictional designations only (no real makers or model names).

- **V-45** - the station's new .45 SMG, issued at the armory. Always "V-45" (plural "V-45s");
  the armorer may add "forty-five calibre". Never "Vector" or a maker's name.

## The district and city

- City: **Port Merrow** (fictional), an old river-port city that lost its industry.
- District: **Carver Street / Saint Abel** - brick tenements, a park nobody maintains,
  boarded shops, one good diner. The flat and the station are both here.
- Local crew: **the Carver Street boys**, small-time dealers who run product out of the
  Garland Arms tenement.

**OPEN:** city and district names are placeholders.

## Tone rules

- Grounded, dry, tired. Realistic like Trepang2's world, not comic.
- Humor comes from money, bureaucracy and fatigue, never from winking at the player.
- Nobody explains the plot. People talk about their day.
- Horror is absent in the first missions. At most one or two deniable hints per location,
  each logged in `foreshadowing.md`.
- No real people, police departments, brands or places.
- Short lines: spoken under ~20 words, objectives under ~8, tips one line.
